🎚️ Permission modes

ModeBehaviour
🟢 Ask (default)Edits in your repo apply; non-read-only shell commands need approval
🟡 Auto-ReviewRules + a reviewer model approve routine calls, refuse high-risk ones
🔴 Full AccessNever asks, sandbox off (repo rules and managed policy still apply)
Per upstream, 0.10.3 will unify this into one Shift+Tab dial (Ask, Accept Edits, Plan, Auto-Review, Full Access). Check docs/MODES.md for your version.
